# Product Vision

> **Bootstrap draft derived from operator architecture brief — pending human review**

Stratifit is an AI-native media and entertainment operating platform. Its purpose is to
make high-quality, AI-assisted media production — film, series, music, comedy,
documentaries, and live programming — systematically plannable, producible, quality
controlled, and publishable at professional standards, by an internal operating team
using an AI-first production pipeline.

The platform separates **creation** from **consumption**:

- **Stratifit Control** is the internal operating system where a small, authorized team
  plans, produces, quality-controls, approves, and publishes content — with AI as a
  governed capability, never as an uncontrolled authority.
- **Stratifit Media** is the public, audience-first destination where everything
  published from the internal ecosystem reaches viewers: films, series, shorts, comedy,
  music, documentaries, live programs, and the AI creators who front them.

Every piece of public content originates from the internal production ecosystem:
Internal creation → Planning → Production → QC → Approval → Publication → Stratifit Media.

AI assists people; it does not replace human authority. Expensive compute is spent only
behind validated plans and explicit gates. Every important generation is reproducible
through provenance. Publishing is a separate domain from production. Public users are
audience, not producers. Humans retain critical authority at every gate.
